How a night goes

You and your crew run an ice cream van by day and a horror channel by night. Sneak into dark buildings, paint yourself in, film what hunts you for views.

Before dark

  1. Clock in at the depotDepot

    Pick up a sponsor deal at the YouCone laptop, buy gear from the vending machine, bolt new parts onto the van. Then everyone piles in.

After dark

  1. Get the lights back onPlant room

    Find the breaker box and do the wires. The generator roars when it starts, and something always hears it.

  2. Paint yourself the colour of the wallAny wall

    Spray your suit to match what is behind you, flatten against it and hold still. The better the match, the closer the Eye can walk past.

  3. Film the scary stuffCamera

    Point the YouCone camera at whatever is out there. Shout your sponsor out while a monster is in the shot, just not too loud: some of them hunt by sound.

Morning

  1. Watch it back togetherDepot TV

    The sponsor's ad first, then your episode. Comments roll in, views turn into money, the quota creeps closer.

Then do it all again.If you are still alive.

Pick who you scream as

The playtest build is free while we find the bugs. Grab it, round up your crew and tell us what scared you.

First time?

  1. Run the installer. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the game is not code-signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway.
  2. One player hosts. Their pause menu shows the address friends join with.
  3. Everyone else picks Join and pastes that address.
  4. Pick a crewmate, take a sponsor deal at the laptop and get in the van.

Updating: run the newer installer over the old one. Your saves stay. Steam is next, once the playtest goes well.
