How a night goes

Here's the plan

1

Clock in at the depot

Pick up a sponsor deal at the YouCone laptop, buy gear from the vending machine and bolt new parts onto the van. Then everyone piles in.

2

Get the lights back on

The building's dark when you arrive. Find the breaker box and do the wires. The generator roars when it starts, and something always hears it.

3

Paint yourself the colour of the wall

Spray your suit to match what's behind you, flatten against it and hold still. The Eye judges what it sees, so the better the match, the closer it can walk past.

4

Film the scary stuff

Point the YouCone camera at whatever's out there. Shout out your sponsor while a monster's in the shot. Just don't shout too loud: some of them hunt by sound.

5

Watch it back together

Back at the depot, the whole crew gathers round the big TV: the sponsor's ad first, then your episode. Comments roll in, views turn into money, and the quota creeps closer.

Then do it all again. If you're still alive.

Who's in the building

Seven reasons to bring a torch

Each one hunts a different way, so no single trick keeps you safe.

Hunts by sight

The Eye

A tall, hunched robot with a searchlight for a head. Match the wall and hold still, or run.

Hunts by sound

The Listener

Blind, on eight legs, covered in speakers. It hears footsteps, doors, generators and you.

Hunts by disguise

The Mimic

It looks just like one of your crew. It even sounds like them. Check twice.

Hunts by vibration

The Floor Crawler

Flat, fast and under everything. It feels you walking. Crouch, or don't move at all.

Hunts by smell

The Sniffer

A blind hound that follows fresh paint. Your camouflage leaves a trail.

Comes from the vents

The Swarm

Thirty bugs pour out of the air vents at once. Stay out of the ducts.

Rare boss

The Warden

Three metres of bad news with keys on its belt. If you hear chains, leave.
